# Microsoft — Round 1: Recruiter Screen

**What this round assesses:** Background, team matching, logistics, and product interest.

## Format

20–30 min phone/video non-technical screen.

## Representative questions

1. Walk me through your background and why you are interested in Microsoft. `anecdotal`
2. What teams or products interest you: Azure, Windows, Xbox, Office, Bing? `anecdotal`
3. Are you open to Redmond vs. NYC or other offices? `anecdotal`
