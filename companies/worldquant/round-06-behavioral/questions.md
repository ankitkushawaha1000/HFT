# WorldQuant — Round 6: Behavioral

**What this round assesses:** Cross-functional collaboration, ownership, and motivation.

## Format

STAR-format behavioral questions with emphasis on engineers working with quant researchers.

## Representative questions

1. Describe a time you explained a complex technical system to a quantitative researcher. `anecdotal`
2. Tell me about balancing research team velocity against production system stability. `inferred`
3. How do you handle a researcher request that creates unacceptable technical risk? `inferred`
4. Tell me about a complex data pipeline you built end to end. What went wrong? `anecdotal`
5. Why WorldQuant vs other quant funds? `anecdotal`
