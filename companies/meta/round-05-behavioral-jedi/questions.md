# Meta — Round 5: Behavioral / Jedi Round

**What this round assesses:** Initiative, impact, collaboration, failure handling, directness, and end-to-end ownership.

## Format

Conversational 45-min values round using STAR-format responses; no coding.

## Representative questions

1. Tell me about the most technically complex project you have worked on. What was your individual contribution? `anecdotal`
2. Describe a time you had to push back on a product direction you disagreed with technically. `anecdotal`
3. Tell me about a time you moved fast and broke something. What happened and what did you do? `anecdotal`
4. Give an example of a time you had to influence peers who did not report to you. `anecdotal`
5. Describe a time you had to trade off short-term delivery against long-term technical health. `anecdotal`
6. Tell me about a project where you had to go end-to-end: design, implement, and maintain something impactful. `anecdotal`
