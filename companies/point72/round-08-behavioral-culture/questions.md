# Point72 Asset Management (incl. Cubist Systematic Strategies) — Round 8: Behavioral / Culture

**What this round assesses:** Collaboration, ownership, disagreement, failure handling, and motivation for systematic trading.

## Format

STAR-format behavioral questions plus motivation/culture discussion.

## Representative questions

1. Describe a technically complex project you owned from design to production. `anecdotal`
2. Tell me about a time you pushed back on a technical approach and were right — and a time you were wrong. `anecdotal`
3. How do you work with quant researchers who have strong opinions about infrastructure? `anecdotal`
4. Describe a time you made a latency–correctness tradeoff. `anecdotal`
5. What excites you about systematic trading vs other software engineering? `anecdotal`
6. Tell me about delivering under a hard deadline with incomplete information. `anecdotal`
