# Google — Round 5: Googleyness & Leadership Behavioral

**What this round assesses:** Leadership, teamwork, conflict resolution, handling failure, ambiguity, and impact beyond assigned work.

## Format

Conversational 45-min video call with structured behavioral questions; no coding.

## Representative questions

1. Tell me about a time you had a significant technical disagreement with a teammate. How did you resolve it? `anecdotal`
2. Describe a project where you had to learn a new technology or domain quickly. `anecdotal`
3. Tell me about a time you made a significant technical mistake. What happened and what did you learn? `anecdotal`
4. How do you handle working with a team member who is not contributing effectively? `anecdotal`
5. Give an example of a time you identified and solved a problem outside your core responsibilities. `anecdotal`
6. Describe a situation where you had incomplete information but had to make a technical decision under time pressure. `anecdotal`
