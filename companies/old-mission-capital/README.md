## Compensation (public estimates)

Public estimates, not offers: levels.fyi, Glassdoor, and Blind suggest Chicago software engineer base salaries around $130–220k, with performance bonus bringing total compensation to about $200–400k+ at senior levels in some reports. These are public estimates only and may be stale or skewed by role and year. `anecdotal — medium confidence`

## Interview process at a glance

| Round | Typical length | What it assesses |
|---|---:|---|
| 1. Recruiter / HR Screen | ~20–30 min | Background, motivation, logistics |
| 2. Online Coding Assessment | ~60–90 min | Algorithms and data structures |
| 3. Technical Phone/Video Screen | ~45–60 min | Coding, C++, data structures, communication |
| 4. Onsite Super Day | ~4–6 hrs | Coding, C++ depth, systems design, trading/probability game, behavioral fit |
