# Akuna Capital — Round 1: Online Assessment

**What this round assesses:** DSA correctness and efficiency; for some trading-technology tracks, speed arithmetic and probability.

## Format

Timed automated assessment, typically 2–3 algorithmic coding problems with a possible mental-math component for trading-track roles.

## Online assessment details

Platform: HackerRank. Number/type: typically 2–3 medium LeetCode-style coding problems. Representative topics: arrays, hashing, sliding window, graph BFS/DFS, and optional arithmetic/probability. Akuna has reported C++ OA formats on CodeSignal/HackerRank; the report specifically cites HackerRank candidate evidence and C++-oriented roles.

## Games / probability

Possible speed arithmetic: fractions, percentages, and simple sequences when the mental-math section appears.

## Representative questions

1. Two-pointer or sliding-window problem on arrays, medium difficulty `anecdotal`
2. Graph traversal problem such as BFS/DFS on a grid or adjacency list `anecdotal`
3. Hash-map-based lookup question `anecdotal`
4. Speed arithmetic: fractions, percentages, simple sequences, if the mental-math section is present `inferred`
