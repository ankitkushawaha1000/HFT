## Compensation (public estimates)

Public estimates, not offers: aggregated levels.fyi and Glassdoor reports suggest entry-to-mid software engineer base salaries around €80–130k in Amsterdam or $130–200k in Chicago/NYC, with significant performance-related bonus. Senior total compensation can be materially higher, sometimes estimated around 1.5–2.5× base in strong years, but public data is sparse and may lag the market. `anecdotal`

## Interview process at a glance

| Round | Typical length | What it assesses |
|---|---:|---|
| 1. Online Assessment | ~90 min | Algorithmic coding, data structures, combinatorial reasoning |
| 2. Technical Phone/Video Screen | ~45–60 min | Live coding, C++ fundamentals, systems basics, communication |
| 3. Onsite / Virtual Onsite | ~3–5 hrs total | Algorithms, C++/systems depth, probability, trading intuition, culture fit |
| 4. Offer/debrief | — | Final decision and compensation discussion |
