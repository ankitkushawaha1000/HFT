# IMC Trading — Round 4: Onsite / Virtual Onsite

**What this round assesses:** Advanced algorithms, C++ systems, probability, market-making intuition, Bayesian updating, risk management, and behavioral fit.

## Format

4–5 rounds over about 4–5 hours. Reported sub-rounds include advanced coding, C++ systems deep dive, IMC's market-making game, probability/quantitative reasoning, and behavioral/culture fit.

## Games / probability

IMC's market-making game is a signature 30–45 minute round. A moderator gives partial information and asks the candidate to make two-sided bid/ask markets. Evaluation centers on spread width, expected value, Bayesian updating, adverse selection, and position/risk management.

## Representative questions

1. "Implement Bellman-Ford. Now modify it to detect negative cycles." `anecdotal`
2. "Design a data structure for a trading system that supports: add order, cancel order, get best bid/ask in O(log N)." `anecdotal`
3. "Write a lock-free ring buffer (single-producer, single-consumer)." `anecdotal`
4. "Explain the C++ object memory model: vtable layout, multiple inheritance diamond problem, memory overhead of virtual functions." `anecdotal`
5. "How does `std::allocator` work? Implement a custom pool allocator for fixed-size objects." `anecdotal`
6. "How does kernel bypass networking (DPDK / Solarflare OpenOnload) improve latency vs. standard socket APIs?" `anecdotal`
7. "I have two dice. I'm going to roll them and tell you the sum is greater than 7. What's your market for the exact sum?" `anecdotal`
8. "I have a card drawn from a standard deck face-down. I'll tell you it's a face card. Make a market in the card's numeric value." `anecdotal`
9. "A random number is drawn uniformly from [0, 100]. I'll reveal to you it's even. Make a market." `anecdotal`
10. "I flip 3 coins. Tell me the result was at least 2 heads. Make a market in the number of heads." `anecdotal`
11. Bayesian updating, expected value calculation, managing adverse selection risk. `inferred`
12. "What is the expected number of times you flip a fair coin before getting 3 consecutive heads?" `anecdotal`
13. "A stock goes up 10% or down 10% with equal probability each day. After 100 days, what is the expected price? What is the median price?" `anecdotal`
14. "Tell me about a time a production system failed and you had to debug it under pressure." `anecdotal`
