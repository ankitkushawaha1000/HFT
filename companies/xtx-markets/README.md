# XTX Markets — Interview Guide

Educational preparation guide for XTX Markets interviews, emphasizing quantitative coding, C++ systems, statistics, ML research, and low-latency infrastructure.

## Compensation (public estimates)

These figures are public estimates from aggregated reports, filings, or cited public sources; they are not offers and can vary materially by role, location, year, and performance.

- Entry or early-career public estimates: highly variable; public reports include very high AI-research intern compensation, with full-time roles estimated competitively against top-tier firms. These are public estimates, not offers.
- Senior public estimates: roughly ~$250–500k+ TC or ~£150k–£400k+ in London for senior roles, depending on track and market. These are public estimates, not offers.

## Interview process at a glance

| Round | Typical length | What it assesses |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Online Assessment (Quantitative / Coding) | 60–120 minutes | Algorithmic coding, quantitative reasoning, data analysis, and statistical thinking. |
| 2. Technical Interview (Coding + Systems / Research) | 60 minutes | C++/Python programming, DSA depth, low-latency systems for SWE, and quantitative research methodology for research roles. |
| 3. Advanced Technical / Research Interview | 60–90 minutes | Expert-level systems knowledge, latency measurement, Linux/networking awareness, ML system design, and research rigour. |
| 4. Final Round (Systems Design + Behavioural) | 45–60 minutes | End-to-end thinking, ownership, communication, and fit with a research-first culture. |
