# Jane Street — Interview Guide

Educational guide to Jane Street's reported software-engineering interview process, emphasizing its official collaborative interview style.

## Snapshot

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| Type | Market maker / global liquidity provider across asset classes |
| HQ | New York, New York, USA |
| Main offices | New York, London, Hong Kong, Amsterdam, Singapore |
| Tech stack | OCaml, Python, C++, systems and networking tooling |
| C++ usage | Used in some systems roles, but OCaml is the dominant internal language; candidates may interview in their strongest language. |
| Career page | https://www.janestreet.com/join-jane-street/software-engineering/ |

## Compensation (public estimates)

These are public estimates from sources such as Glassdoor, levels.fyi, Blind, and Reddit, not offers or official ranges.

| Level | Public estimate |
|---|---|
| Entry / general SWE | Base salary reported around $150K–$250K. |
| Senior / high-performing IC | Total compensation, including discretionary profit share, frequently reported around $300K–$600K+ and can vary significantly with firm performance. |

## Interview process at a glance

| Round | Typical length | What it assesses |
|---|---:|---|
| Recruiter / initial screen | 15–30 min | Culture interest, background fit, logistics, and motivation. |
| Technical phone screens | Usually 2 rounds, 45–60 min each | Collaborative live coding, problem solving, communication, and reasoning. |
| Superday / Onsite | 4–6 interviews | Coding, design, behavioral fit, abstraction, decomposition, and collaborative reasoning. |
