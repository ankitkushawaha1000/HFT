# Jane Street — Round 2: Technical Phone Screens

**What this round assesses:** Collaborative live coding, problem solving, communication, clarifying questions, and how the candidate thinks through problems.

## Format

Typically two 45–60 minute technical phone screens. Jane Street is reported not to use a standard online assessment platform before phone screens for software engineers.

## Representative questions

1. Write a function to find all permutations of a string, or solve a similar combinatorics/recursion problem. `[anecdotal]`
2. Implement a functional-style map/filter/reduce pipeline in your language of choice. `[anecdotal]`
3. How does copy elision work, and why is return-by-value often the right design today? `[inferred]`
4. Explain `std::expected` / `std::optional` and how you would use them to model error handling. `[inferred]`
5. Implement binary search correctly, then generalize it to find the leftmost insertion point. `[anecdotal]`
6. Solve a graph problem such as finding a shortest path with constraints or detecting a cycle in a directed graph. `[anecdotal]`
7. Design a simple rate limiter. What data structures would you use? `[anecdotal]`
8. Probability/math puzzle: Two people take turns flipping a fair coin; the first to get heads wins. What is the probability the first player wins? `[anecdotal]`
9. Given a deck of N cards, you draw until a face card appears. What is the expected number of draws? `[anecdotal]`
