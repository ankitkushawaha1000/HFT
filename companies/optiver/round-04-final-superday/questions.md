# Optiver — Round 4: Final Round / Superday

**What this round assesses:** Seniority calibration, values alignment, production ownership, breadth across systems/reliability/collaboration, and sustained quality under scrutiny.

## Format

A half-day block of multiple back-to-back interviews. Senior candidates may also meet engineering leaders.

## Representative questions

1. Tell me about a time you led a performance improvement effort without directly managing the engineers involved. `[inferred]`
2. Give an example of when you had to align technical and business stakeholders who wanted different outcomes. `[inferred]`
3. Tell me about the worst production incident you handled. `[inferred]`
4. Tell me about a major architecture decision you influenced, and what tradeoffs you made. `[inferred]`
5. Describe a technical disagreement with your manager or team — how did you resolve it? `[inferred]`
6. Tell me about a failure that changed how you work. `[inferred]`
7. Why Optiver rather than a generic infrastructure or FAANG role? `[inferred]`
8. How have you mentored someone who was struggling? `[inferred]`
