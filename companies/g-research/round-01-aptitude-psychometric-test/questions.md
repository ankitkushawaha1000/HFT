# G-Research — Round 1: Aptitude / Psychometric Test

**What this round assesses:** Cognitive ability, numerical speed, logical reasoning, and abstract pattern recognition.

## Format

Standardised online psychometric battery before coding assessment, often described as logical, numerical, spatial, or abstract reasoning.

## Online assessment details

Platform: SHL or equivalent psychometric platform. Number/type: third-party aptitude battery with numerical reasoning, logical/verbal reasoning, abstract patterns, and diagrammatic reasoning.

## Games / probability

Aptitude/numerical test with percentage-change calculations, data interpretation, ratios, series completion, syllogisms, shape sequences, and symbol transformations.

## Representative questions

1. Numerical reasoning: percentage-change calculations, data interpretation from tables, and ratio problems. `anecdotal`
2. Logical/verbal reasoning: series completion, syllogisms, and conditional logic. `anecdotal`
3. Abstract patterns: identify which shape completes the sequence. `anecdotal`
4. Diagrammatic reasoning: infer rules governing symbol transformations. `anecdotal`
