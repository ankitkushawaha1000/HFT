# Maven Securities — Round 4: Onsite in London

**What this round assesses:** Coding, C++ depth, probability, trading intuition, and behavioral fit for an ETP market maker.

## Format

On-site panels reported to include coding, C++ technical depth, probability/trading intuition, and behavioral interviews. `anecdotal — low confidence`

## Representative questions

1. Design and implement an order book supporting add order, cancel order, and best bid/ask query in O(1). `inferred`
2. Find the longest substring without repeating characters. `anecdotal/general-prep`
3. Explain virtual dispatch in C++, including vtables and overhead. `inferred/general-prep`
4. How does `std::shared_ptr` manage reference counts in a thread-safe way, and what is the cost? `inferred`
5. You are making markets on a coin flip and offer 45/55; your counterparty accepts one side. What information does that give you, and how do you adjust? `inferred`
6. A random walk starts at 0 and moves +1 or -1 with equal probability; what is the expected time to reach +10 or -10? `general-prep/anecdotal`
7. Tell me about a time you shipped something under significant time pressure and what shortcuts you made or refused to make. `general-prep`
