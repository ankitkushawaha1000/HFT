# D.E. Shaw & Co. — Round 8: Superday — System Design

**What this round assesses:** Architecture judgment, latency/correctness/maintainability tradeoffs, and production systems experience.

## Format

Whiteboard/discussion round for large-scale or performance-critical system design.

## Representative questions

1. Design a distributed rate limiter for 1M requests/second across 10 servers and handle clock skew. `anecdotal`
2. Design real-time market data processing from raw feeds to 200 consumers at sub-microsecond fan-out. `inferred`
3. Design a persistent key-value store with crash recovery and sub-millisecond reads. `anecdotal`
4. Design a circuit breaker for an algorithmic trading system exceeding real-time risk limits. `inferred`
5. Design a time-series database for 5 years of tick data across 50,000 instruments. `anecdotal`
6. Design a fair, deadlock-free high-throughput scheduler for 1,000 compute nodes. `anecdotal`
