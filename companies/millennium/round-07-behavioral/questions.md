# Millennium Management — Round 7: Behavioral

**What this round assesses:** Ownership, collaboration, handling failure, and motivation.

## Format

Structured behavioral interview; STAR format expected.

## Representative questions

1. Tell me about a time you optimized a system for performance under a hard deadline. `anecdotal`
2. Describe a situation where you strongly disagreed with a technical decision. `anecdotal`
3. Give an example of coordinating across multiple teams with conflicting priorities. `anecdotal`
4. Why move from your current environment to a quant fund? `anecdotal`
5. Describe a production incident where you were involved in root-cause analysis. `anecdotal`
6. Tell me about a time you made a consequential technical decision with incomplete information. `anecdotal`
