# Microsoft — Round 5: Hiring Manager / Behavioral

**What this round assesses:** Collaboration, learning orientation, ambiguity, leadership, and Microsoft Growth Mindset alignment.

## Format

45–60 min conversational no-coding round with hiring manager.

## Representative questions

1. Tell me about a time you had to learn a new technology quickly and apply it to production. `anecdotal`
2. Describe a time you influenced a cross-team decision without direct authority. `anecdotal`
3. Tell me about a significant project failure. What did you learn and what would you do differently? `anecdotal`
4. How do you handle receiving critical feedback on your code? `anecdotal`
5. Describe the most technically ambiguous problem you had to solve and how you structured it. `anecdotal`
6. Tell me about a time you balanced technical debt with feature delivery deadlines. `anecdotal`
