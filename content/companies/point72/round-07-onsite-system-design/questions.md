# Point72 Asset Management (incl. Cubist Systematic Strategies) — Round 7: On-site — System Design

**What this round assesses:** Architecture judgment, tradeoff defense, and production thinking.

## Format

Whiteboard/diagram discussion for trading or infrastructure systems under latency, throughput, fault-tolerance, and correctness constraints.

## Representative questions

1. Design an end-to-end trade execution system from signal generation to order submission and position tracking. `inferred`
2. Design a market data fan-out system delivering normalized ticks from 20 exchanges to 50 subscribers. `anecdotal`
3. Design a real-time P&L engine reacting to 100K events/second with sub-millisecond latency. `inferred`
4. Design a distributed back-testing framework replaying 10 years of tick data at 100x speed with deterministic ordering. `inferred`
5. Describe pre-trade risk checks: what data is needed, how is it kept fast, and how does it fail safely? `anecdotal`
