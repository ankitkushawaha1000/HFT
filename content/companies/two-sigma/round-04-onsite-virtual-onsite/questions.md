# Two Sigma — Round 4: Onsite / Virtual Onsite

**What this round assesses:** Advanced algorithms, deep C++/systems knowledge, distributed/system design, probability/statistics, and behavioral fit.

## Format

4–5 back-to-back rounds over about 4–5 hours. Reported sub-rounds include algorithms, C++/systems programming, systems design, probability/statistics or research thinking, and behavioral/hiring manager discussion.

## Representative questions

1. "Implement Dijkstra's algorithm and explain its time complexity." `anecdotal`
2. "Given a list of transactions, find the minimum number of transfers to settle all debts." `anecdotal`
3. "Implement a skip list and explain why you'd use it over a balanced BST." `anecdotal`
4. "Design a data structure that supports insert, delete, and getRandom in O(1)." `anecdotal`
5. "Explain move semantics. What is `std::move` and what does it actually do at the ASM level? What is `std::forward` and when must you use it?" `anecdotal`
6. "What are memory orderings (`std::memory_order_relaxed`, `_acquire`, `_release`, `_seq_cst`)? Write a lock-free stack using atomics." `anecdotal`
7. "What is false sharing? Write a struct that would exhibit false sharing and then fix it." `anecdotal`
8. "Implement a compile-time Fibonacci using `constexpr` and/or template metaprogramming." `anondotal`
9. "Design a real-time market data feed handler that can process 1M messages/second." `anecdotal`
10. "Design a backtesting framework that can replay 10 years of tick data." `anecdotal`
11. "What is the expected number of coin flips to get two consecutive heads?" `anecdotal`
12. "A biased coin has an unknown probability p of heads. How do you create a fair 50/50 event from it?" `anecdotal`
13. "Describe a project where you had to make a significant technical trade-off. What did you choose and why?" `anecdotal`
