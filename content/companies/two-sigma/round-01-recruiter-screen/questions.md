# Two Sigma — Round 1: Recruiter Screen

**What this round assesses:** Background, team fit, expectations, communication, and basic motivation.

## Format

30-minute phone or video call. Usually behavioral and logistical, with no technical questions. Some candidates report a brief discussion of a system they built.

## Representative questions

1. "Walk me through your resume." `anecdotal`
2. "Why Two Sigma over other quant funds?" `anecdotal`
3. "What kind of engineering problems excite you?" `anecdotal`
4. "Are you comfortable with Python and C++?" `anecdotal`
5. "What are your compensation expectations / location preferences?" `anecdotal`
