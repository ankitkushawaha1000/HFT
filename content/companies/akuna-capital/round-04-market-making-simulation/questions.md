# Akuna Capital — Round 4: Market-Making Simulation / Trading Game

**What this round assesses:** Real-time EV thinking, quoting discipline, inventory management, spread adjustment, and calm under adverse selection.

## Format

Live or structured market-making exercise involving two-sided quotes, simulated assets, inventory, and pricing updates.

## Games / probability

Trading game / market-making simulation using coin, card, option-pricing, or inventory scenarios.

## Representative questions

1. Here is a coin. I will flip it; quote a market where heads pays $1, then update your market after reveals. `anecdotal`
2. You are making a market in a stock and have bought 500 lots. The market moves against you. What spread do you quote now, and how does inventory affect it? `anecdotal`
3. We play a card game where you can buy or sell a number drawn from the deck at my quote. How do you handle a large long position? `anecdotal`
4. A stock is at $100; you think it has 20% chance of going to $120 and 80% chance of staying at $100. What is a $110 call worth? `anecdotal`
