# Five Rings Capital — Round 2: Phone Screen (Probability + Logical Reasoning)

**What this round assesses:** First-principles probability reasoning, mental math, verbal structure, and ability to reason aloud.

## Format

Conversational trader/researcher screen with probability problems, brainteasers, and some mental math; coding-track formats may differ.

## Games / probability

Probability and mental-math screen: puzzles, expected values, conditional probabilities, and fast arithmetic without calculators.

## Representative questions

1. Three ants sit at the corners of an equilateral triangle; each independently picks clockwise or counterclockwise. What is the probability no two ants collide? `anecdotal`
2. You have a bag of 50 red and 50 blue marbles. Blindly draw one. It is red. Without replacement, what is the probability the next draw is red? `anecdotal`
3. Expected number of cards to draw from a 52-card shuffled deck before seeing the first king. `anecdotal`
4. I have a biased coin. Without knowing its bias, how do I generate a fair 50/50 outcome? `anecdotal`
5. 100 prisoners, 100 boxes with random slips, each may open 50. What is the optimal strategy? `anecdotal`
6. Break a stick at two random points. What is the probability the three pieces form a triangle? `anecdotal`
7. If I offered you $1M or a 50% chance of $3M, which would you take, and why does it depend on utility? `anecdotal`
8. You are a market maker quoting a coin flip: pays $1 on heads, nothing on tails. If I keep asking to transact at your quote, how do you adjust as inventory accumulates? `inferred`
9. Without a calculator: 37 × 43 = ? `anecdotal`
10. What is 13/16 as a decimal? `anecdotal`
