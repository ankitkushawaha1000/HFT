# Five Rings Capital — Round 4: Trading Game (EV Decisions)

**What this round assesses:** EV calculation speed, calm under adversity, bidding discipline, and real-time position risk management.

## Format

Live market-making or trading game: cards, coins, dice, two-sided markets, and optimal stopping.

## Games / probability

Interactive EV game or market-making simulation with immediate quote and risk decisions.

## Representative questions

1. A deck of 52 cards is shuffled. Red cards = +$1 and black cards = −$1. You draw one at a time and can stop whenever. What is the optimal strategy and expected value? `anecdotal`
2. I will roll a 100-sided die. You set a price. I will buy 100 units at your ask or sell 100 units at your bid. You have 5 seconds per quote. `anecdotal`
