# Five Rings Capital — Round 1: Online Assessment (Probability + Puzzles)

**What this round assesses:** Probability intuition, combinatorics, logical reasoning, puzzles, and speed under uncertainty.

## Format

Timed online test with probability/brainteasers and possibly some coding; less algorithm-heavy than pure-engineering OAs.

## Online assessment details

Platform: reports vary between HackerRank and proprietary assessment. Number/type: mixed probability, puzzles, and possible coding. Representative topics: expected value games, conditional probability, coin-flip sequences, combinatorics, and geometric-series paradoxes.

## Games / probability

Probability and mental-math emphasis is central: EV games, Bayes, coin sequences, combinatorics, and fast arithmetic.

## Representative questions

1. Expected value of a game: you can roll a die repeatedly; you get paid the final shown value but pay $0.50 each re-roll. What is the optimal strategy and expected value? `anecdotal`
2. Conditional probability: Bayes theorem application with a rare disease test `anecdotal`
3. Coin flip sequence: expected flips to see HH vs HT `anecdotal`
4. Combinatorics: how many distinct binary strings of length n have no two consecutive 1s? `anecdotal`
5. Geometric series / expected value: a fair coin pays $2^n if n flips to first head — why would you not pay $50 for this game? `anecdotal`
