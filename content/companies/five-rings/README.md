## Interview process at a glance

| Round | Typical length | What it assesses |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Online Assessment (Probability + Puzzles) | 45–75 minutes | Probability intuition, combinatorics, logical reasoning, puzzles, and speed under uncertainty. |
| 2. Phone Screen (Probability + Logical Reasoning) | 45–60 minutes | First-principles probability reasoning, mental math, verbal structure, and ability to reason aloud. |
| 3. Onsite / Virtual Day (Games + Talk-Through) | Multiple 45–60 minute sessions | Deeper probability, communication under pressure, EV-based decision-making, and for developer-track candidates C++ and data structures. |
| 4. Trading Game (EV Decisions) | 30–60 minutes | EV calculation speed, calm under adversity, bidding discipline, and real-time position risk management. |
