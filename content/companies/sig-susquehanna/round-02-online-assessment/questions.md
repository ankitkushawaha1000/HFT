# SIG (Susquehanna International Group) — Round 2: Online Assessment

**What this round assesses:** Algorithmic coding, math/probability-style coding, and domain-flavored simulation.

## Format

HackerRank assessment with 2–3 coding problems over 60–90 minutes. Reports describe LeetCode medium-to-hard difficulty and strong algorithmic focus.

## Online assessment details

- **Platform:** HackerRank.
- **Problems:** 2–3 coding problems.
- **Representative OA topics:** Options-trade simulation, DP, number theory, probability-adjacent card-game simulation, graph traversal, expression evaluation.

## Games / probability

Some reported OA problems are probability-adjacent, such as simulating a card game and computing a winning probability.

## Representative questions

1. "Given a list of options trades (strike, expiry, type, quantity), compute the net delta exposure." `anecdotal`
2. Dynamic programming: counting paths, coin change variants. `anecdotal`
3. Number theory: "Find all prime numbers in a range" using Sieve of Eratosthenes. `anecdotal`
4. Probability-adjacent coding: "Simulate a card game and compute the probability of winning." `anecdotal`
5. Graph algorithm: BFS/DFS variant. `anecdotal`
6. "Implement a basic expression evaluator (with +, -, *, /, parentheses)." `anecdotal`
