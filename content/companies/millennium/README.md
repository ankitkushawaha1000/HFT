## Interview process at a glance

| Round | Step | Typical length | What it assesses |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | HR / Recruiter Screen | 25–35 min | Communication clarity, motivation, location/visa fit, and high-level technical background. |
| 2 | Online Assessment (OA) | ~90 min | Correctness and efficiency of algorithmic solutions under time pressure. |
| 3 | Technical Phone Screen | 60 min | Live coding, C++ language depth, and basic systems knowledge. |
| 4 | On-site — Coding / Algorithms | 45–60 min | Problem decomposition, clean implementation, complexity reasoning, and edge-case handling. |
| 5 | On-site — C++ and Systems Depth | 45–60 min | C++ mastery, undefined behavior, atomics, cache effects, and production performance reasoning. |
| 6 | On-site — Domain / Trading Systems Design | 45–60 min | Trading infrastructure knowledge, latency-aware design, and order lifecycle understanding. |
| 7 | Behavioral | 30–45 min | Ownership, collaboration, handling failure, and motivation. |
