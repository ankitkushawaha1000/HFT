# Virtu Financial — Round 1: Online Assessment (Probability / Coding)

**What this round assesses:** Basic DSA fluency, EV reasoning, and market-making intuition.

## Format

Timed OA with 1–2 algorithmic coding problems and some probability or market-making scenario questions.

## Online assessment details

Platform: HackerRank or similar. Number/type: usually 1–2 algorithmic coding problems plus probability or market-making scenarios. Representative topics: strings/arrays, hash maps, sorting, spread-capture calculations, and basic coin probability.

## Representative questions

1. String or array manipulation problem at LeetCode-medium level `anecdotal`
2. Hash-map or sorting problem `anecdotal`
3. A market maker buys and sells a stock. If the bid-ask spread is $0.10 and they trade 1 million shares/day, what is gross revenue and what risks eat into it? `inferred`
4. Basic probability: probability of exactly 2 heads in 4 coin flips `anecdotal`
