# Virtu Financial — Round 3: Quoting Simulation / Inventory Management

**What this round assesses:** Real-time EV, risk management, quote adjustment with inventory, and adverse-selection awareness.

## Format

Live simulation or worked exercise requiring two-sided quotes, inventory management, and pricing updates.

## Games / probability

Quoting simulation focused on market-making and inventory management.

## Representative questions

1. You are making a market in a stock at $100. Your current position is long 10,000 shares. How do you adjust your bid and ask, and why? `inferred`
2. The bid-ask on an ETF is $100.00 / $100.02. A large sell order comes in. How does this affect your quote on the underlying components? `inferred`
3. Model the P&L of a market maker over 1,000 trades with a 50% fill rate and $0.01 spread. What is the expected P&L distribution and its risk factors? `inferred`
