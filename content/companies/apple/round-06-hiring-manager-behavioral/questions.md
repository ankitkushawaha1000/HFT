# Apple — Round 6: Hiring Manager / Behavioral Round

**What this round assesses:** Technical background depth, team fit, communication clarity, and ownership of past work.

## Format

1–2 conversational rounds, 45–60 min each; less LP-structured than Amazon and focused on deep work ownership.

## Representative questions

1. Tell me about the most complex C++ system you have designed and owned. What are you most proud of? `anecdotal`
2. How do you approach performance optimization? Walk through a real profiling and hotspot fix. `anecdotal`
3. Describe a time you made a significant architectural decision. Who did you consult and how did you evaluate options? `anecdotal`
4. Why Apple? What draws you to the product ecosystem rather than other companies? `anecdotal`
5. How do you handle working under strict secrecy or NDA constraints on product features? `anecdotal`
6. Tell me about a time your code had a serious production bug. How did you diagnose and fix it? `anecdotal`
