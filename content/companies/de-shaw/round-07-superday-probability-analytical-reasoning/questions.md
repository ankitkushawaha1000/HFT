# D.E. Shaw & Co. — Round 7: Superday — Probability / Analytical Reasoning

**What this round assesses:** Probabilistic thinking, first-principles reasoning, mental arithmetic, and Bayesian reasoning.

## Format

Dedicated probability/statistics/quantitative reasoning discussion; no coding.

## Representative questions

1. Two eggs, 100 floors: find the minimum worst-case number of drops. `anecdotal`
2. Expected number of coin flips to get two consecutive heads. `anecdotal`
3. Expected rolls until all six faces of a die have appeared. `anecdotal`
4. Random walk from 0 with ±1 steps: expected steps to reach position N. `anecdotal`
5. Two people roll dice; probability your number is strictly greater. `anecdotal`
6. Bayes bag problem: after drawing a red ball, probability you picked the first bag. `anecdotal`
7. Rare-disease test with 99% sensitivity/specificity: probability of disease after a positive result. `anecdotal`
8. Expected shuffles of N cards until sorted order appears. `anecdotal`
9. 3x3 grid paths from top-left to bottom-right and expected steps. `anecdotal`
10. Optimal strategy for calling 'now' before the ace of spades appears. `anecdotal`
11. Three people pick numbers 1 to 6; probability all three are distinct. `anecdotal`
12. Coin game with heads +$2 and tails -$1: expected value and whether you would play. `anecdotal`
