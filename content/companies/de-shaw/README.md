## Interview process at a glance

| Round | Step | Typical length | What it assesses |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recruiter Screen | 30 min | Fit, communication, and technical background credibility. |
| 2 | Online Assessment (OA) | ~90 min | Algorithmic problem-solving efficiency and correctness. |
| 3 | Technical Phone/Video Screen(s) | 60 min each | Live coding ability, C++ depth, and communication under pressure. |
| 4 | Superday — Coding Round 1 | 45–60 min | Algorithmic thinking, clean C++ code, edge cases, and complexity. |
| 5 | Superday — Coding Round 2 | 45–60 min | Algorithmic depth and ability to manage multiple constraints. |
| 6 | Superday — C++ / Systems Depth | 45–60 min | Expert C++ and systems knowledge for production HFT/trading infrastructure. |
| 7 | Superday — Probability / Analytical Reasoning | 45–60 min | Probabilistic thinking, first-principles reasoning, mental arithmetic, and Bayesian reasoning. |
| 8 | Superday — System Design | 45–60 min | Architecture judgment, latency/correctness/maintainability tradeoffs, and production systems experience. |
| 9 | Superday — Behavioral / Culture | 30–45 min | Technical ownership, collaboration, handling complexity, and motivation. |
