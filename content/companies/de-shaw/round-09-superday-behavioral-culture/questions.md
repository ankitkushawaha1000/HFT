# D.E. Shaw & Co. — Round 9: Superday — Behavioral / Culture

**What this round assesses:** Technical ownership, collaboration, handling complexity, and motivation.

## Format

Discussion-based behavioral round, often with a senior engineer or team lead.

## Representative questions

1. Tell me about the most technically complex system you built and the hardest design decisions. `anecdotal`
2. Describe a time you identified a critical production bug. `anecdotal`
3. Tell me about convincing a senior engineer to change their technical approach. `anecdotal`
4. What is the most valuable thing you learned from a production incident? `anecdotal`
5. Why work at a firm combining technology and investing at this depth? `anecdotal`
6. What engineering problems do you find most intellectually engaging? `anecdotal`
7. Describe delivering a project under a hard constraint and the tradeoffs you made. `anecdotal`
