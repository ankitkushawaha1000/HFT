# D.E. Shaw & Co. — Round 6: Superday — C++ / Systems Depth

**What this round assesses:** Expert C++ and systems knowledge for production HFT/trading infrastructure.

## Format

Deep technical discussion on C++ mechanics, Linux systems, memory, and concurrency.

8. How does the Linux scheduler interact with std::thread? `anecdotal`
