# Optiver — Round 2: Online Assessment / Technical Assessment

**What this round assesses:** Coding fluency under time pressure, CS fundamentals, ambiguous or longer-form problem solving, and occasionally reasoning or logic tasks.

## Format

Typically 90 minutes to several hours. The assessment is broader than a pure LeetCode-style screen and may include implementation-heavy tasks.

## Online assessment details

- **Platform:** HackerRank is most commonly reported; some roles may use an Optiver-internal platform.
- **Number/type of problems:** 2–4 medium-hard coding problems, often with longer realistic prompts; possibly one OOP or implementation-heavy task.
- **Representative OA topics:** Data structures such as stack-with-min, LRU cache, or priority-queue variants; string/array edge cases; BFS/DFS; simple simulation or event processing; occasionally concurrency or shared-state reasoning.
