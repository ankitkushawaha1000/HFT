
# Behavioral Foundations

Senior behavioral interviews are usually judgment interviews in disguise. The interviewer wants evidence that you can operate calmly, influence others, and make tradeoffs when information is incomplete.

## Core Story Types

- A production incident where you stabilized the system under pressure.
- A disagreement with a stakeholder where you changed the decision with evidence.
- A project where you improved reliability, developer speed, or latency.
- A mistake you owned and corrected.
- A mentoring example that shows leverage beyond your individual output.

## Answer Pattern

1. **Situation:** name the environment, stakes, and relevant constraint.
2. **Task:** explain what you personally owned.
3. **Action:** describe your decision process, not just your effort.
4. **Result:** quantify impact and name what changed permanently.
5. **Reflection:** state what you would repeat or improve next time.

## Senior Signals

Strong senior answers usually mention one or more of the following:

- How you narrowed scope under time pressure.
- How you communicated risk to non-experts.
- What instrumentation or evidence changed the decision.
- How you prevented recurrence rather than only fixing symptoms.
