
# Mock Interview Guide

The point of a mock interview is not only to see whether you know the answer. It is to see whether you can retrieve and structure it under pressure.

## How to Use the Timer

- **2 minutes:** flash drill for direct, high-signal answers.
- **5 minutes:** short technical explanation with one follow-up.
- **15 minutes:** focused topic round.
- **30-60 minutes:** realistic interview block.

## Self-Review Questions

- Did I answer the actual question first?
- Did I state tradeoffs rather than listing unrelated facts?
- Did I sound decisive without overstating certainty?
- Could an interviewer probe one level deeper and still get a solid answer?
