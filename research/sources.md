## 6. Anecdotal sources policy

Anecdotal candidate reports were consulted in aggregated form only for pattern awareness, especially when official interview detail was sparse. These sources are **not individually cited** because:

- identity and role scope are difficult to verify;
- process details become stale quickly;
- anecdotal sources often overfit to one office, one year, or one interviewer.

In this repository, anecdotal patterns may influence **Low** or occasionally **Medium** confidence conclusions, but they are never used as sole support for a strong claim.
